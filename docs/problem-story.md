# The problem, in plain words

## One sentence

A factory finds a defect. Three teams blame each other. Weeks pass before anyone proves the cause. The launch waits.

## Words we do not use on stage

- MIL means Major Issues List. Say "the problem list" first. Name MIL once, after the plain words land.
- NPI means New Product Introduction. Say "the first builds before launch."
- FA means Failure Analysis. Say "teardown tests on a few units."
- Yield means the share of units that pass. Say "good units out of 100."

## The factory scenes (pick one per demo, all true to life)

1. **The cold morning.** The first units of the day fail. Units pass after lunch. The team blames the part lot. The parts test fine on the bench. The true cause hides in a cold start that no bench test repeats.
2. **The two stations.** The same unit passes on Station A and fails on Station B. Each station team trusts its own machine. The defect follows the station, not the unit. Nobody owns the space between the two claims.
3. **The new lot.** Good units drop the day a new part lot arrives. The rate stays low after that day. Material takes the blame. The lot only ran on cold mornings, so the lot and the cold share one signature.
4. **The fix that closes and reopens.** A team widens a test limit. Yield jumps. The problem item closes. The same defect returns on the next build, now with less data and more confidence.
5. **The scarce teardown.** Engineers can tear down five units this week, not fifty. Each wrong pick burns a day. The worst-looking units often hold a simple physical defect. The common cause hides in normal-looking units.

## What the judge must remember after 30 seconds

- The parts were fine.
- The proof was scarce.
- The close was fake until a new build proved the fix.

## How Closeout answers each scene

- It reads every test log in one pass and scores each cause.
- It spends teardown picks on units that split the open causes best.
- A second, blind reviewer tries to prove the answer wrong.
- It blocks a close that only widens a limit.
- It closes the item only after a fresh build passes.
